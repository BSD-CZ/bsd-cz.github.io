## Our mission

FreeBSD is a rock-solid operating system, but hardware support sometimes lags behind Linux. We aim to close that gap by:

1. Writing drivers for unsupported hardware
2. Testing and documenting FreeBSD on modern machines
3. Building embedded FreeBSD images for ARM and RISC-V platforms
4. Contributing patches upstream to the FreeBSD project

Most of that work now runs through [SoCBSD]({{ '/projects/socbsd.html' | relative_url }}), a FreeBSD fork dedicated to SoC board bring-up, and through our [ports tree]({{ '/ports/' | relative_url }}).

## Why FreeBSD?

- **Clean codebase** — consistent style, well-documented kernel interfaces
- **Licensing** — permissive BSD license, friendly to commercial and embedded use
- **Networking** — FreeBSD's network stack remains one of the best in the industry
- **ZFS** — first-class ZFS support out of the box
- **Jails & bhyve** — lightweight virtualization without the bloat

## How to join

There is no application form. Pick a board you physically own, check what has already been done, and open an arena issue with a photo of the board and a serial capture of what it currently does.

Not a bring-up person? A board still needs its edge cases hunted, its man pages written, its boot logs verified, and its code reviewed by someone holding the same hardware. All of it counts.

<div class="callout" markdown="1">
**One rule above the others:** contributions are accepted only for hardware you actually have, in working condition, and every claim ships with a serial log. Untested code is how boards end up half-supported forever.
</div>
